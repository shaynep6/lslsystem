Secondary Education

Junior High School & Senior High School

Lyceum of Southern Luzon, Inc. provides information for students exploring junior and senior high school education. Contact the school to confirm current enrollment availability and offerings.

Grade 7

Begin your secondary education with foundational academic subjects, learning activities, and personal development.

Grade 8

Strengthen your knowledge and skills through continued academic learning and student activities.

Grade 9

Develop critical thinking, practical skills, and readiness for the next stage of education.

Grade 10

Complete junior high school and prepare for senior high school pathways and future goals.

HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences. Explore communication, society, culture, history, and human behavior.

ABM

Accountancy, Business, and Management. Learn about business operations, accounting, entrepreneurship, and management.

GAS

General Academic Strand. Build a broad academic foundation for different college and career pathways.

TVL

Technical-Vocational-Livelihood. Develop practical and career-oriented skills through applicable technical and vocational specializations.

Please contact the school to confirm which grade levels, strands, and specializations are available for the current school year.
